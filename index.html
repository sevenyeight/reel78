Drop Shot
Palomar knot leaving a tag end; weight hangs 6-18" below. Keep semi-slack and shake with your wrist. Let it sit — most bites come on the pause. Best in cold/pressured water 10-25ft deep.
BassFinesseClear waterCastaicPyramid
Swim Bait
Steady retrieve at target depth. Match head weight to depth: 1/4oz shallows, 1oz+ for 20ft+. Vary speed until you find the trigger. White/silver in clear water, darker in murky. Countdown to depth before retrieving.
Striped BassLargemouthOpen waterCastaic
Topwater
Most effective dawn/dusk and overcast days. Walk the dog with slack-line twitches. Pause after a blow-up — bass often come back. Use 15lb+ line to horse fish away from cover fast.
BassDawnDuskShallow structure
Texas Rig
Bullet weight above a worm hook. Weedless — punch through grass, brush, rocks. Flip into tight cover. Slow drag along bottom, feel for taps. 1/2-1oz for flipping, 3/16oz for open water.
BassCoverFlipping
Pier / Bottom Fishing
Sliding sinker rig with 2-3ft leader. Squid, cut anchovy, and pile worms all work at Malibu/SM Pier. Fish tide changes — first two hours of incoming or outgoing. Drop near pilings for halibut.
HalibutPerchMalibu PierTides
Surf Casting
Fish the troughs and cuts between sandbars. High-low rig with sand crabs or bloodworms for perch. Corbina take sand crabs in the wash at low tide. Cast past the break, retrieve slowly through the trough.
CorbinaPerchSurfSand crabs
Jig
Lift-and-fall along structure. Let it hit bottom, pop 1-2ft, let fall on semi-slack line — bites on the fall. Match color to clarity: natural in clear, chartreuse in murky. Great for calico bass in kelp.
